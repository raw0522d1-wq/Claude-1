What this tool is

A selective decision engine — not a signal firehose

It only marks a setup when a full cause-and-effect chain completes on a closed bar. Everything on the chart exists to answer one question: is this a high-confluence trade, or should it be rejected?

HTF bias→ liquidity→ session→ sweep→ reclaim→ displacement→ MSS→ entry array→ target & risk

Chapter 2 · How a signal forms
Confirmed long teal triangle, below bar
Confirmed short red triangle, above bar
Developing circle — watch, don't trade

Price wicks below the level (the raid), closes back above it (the reclaim), then a strong bar breaks structure. The triangle only prints at bar close — it never repaints or moves later.

Chapter 4 · The confluence score

0–100 quality rank, in five bands

< 60 REJ
rejected / informational
60–69 DEV
developing, not tradable
70–79 B
qualified B setup
80–89 A
A setup — default threshold 80
90–100 A+
A+ — the rare, cleanest ones

The score is a quality rank, not a probability. A high score never overrides a missing mandatory gate — a setup with no confirmed structure shift is rejected no matter how it scores.

Chapter 5 · The five setups

1 · Turtle Soup

Liquidity raid, reclaim, and reversal off a swept level.

2 · Judas Swing

False move off the session open that gets reclaimed.

3 · FVG Continuation

Pullback into a fresh gap in the direction of bias.

4 · Breaker Retest

Failed order block, flipped, retested from the far side.

5 · SMT Reversal

External raid where the correlated market fails to confirm.

The Setup row names which one fired. Each has its own mandatory gates — all must pass before a score is even computed.

Chapter 6 · How to use it, live

Is trading allowed? If that row says NO, the session, day, or a blackout is gating you — nothing will fire.

Does bias agree? Check HTF bias and Premium/Discount line up with the direction you'd take.

Watch it build. Sweep pending → Reclaim ctx are the developing stages. Don't act yet.

Wait for the triangle. On bar close, read Setup, Score/class, and the R:R.

Take the levels as given. Entry, Stop, and Targets are printed — the indicator does the geometry.

You place the trade. The indicator never sends orders — it's an analyst, not the broker.

Before you trade it

Read the dashboard warnings row every time

It flags a mismatched timeframe, missing SMT data, weak correlation, or a synthetic chart. And remember: validate on Bar Replay and a backtest before risking money — the engine is selective by design, but selectivity isn't the same as proven profit.
