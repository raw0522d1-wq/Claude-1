A daily rhythm for husbands: three tasks, a morning Proverbs prayer, reminders, a streak, and a journal.

Need help or found a problem?
Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Settings in the app and make sure Nudge me every 2 hours is on, then check that notifications are allowed for The Gardener in your phone's settings. Reminders stop for the day once all three tasks are checked off.

How does the streak work?

A day counts when all three of its tasks are checked off. Tend today's tasks to keep the streak growing. Missing a day starts it over, but your longest streak is always kept.

Can I change the names in the prayers?

Yes. Open Settings and edit your name, your wife's name, or your children's names. Leave children blank and the tasks adjust for a home without kids.

Where is my data kept?

Only on your phone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

How do I start over?

Settings → Erase everything. This clears your streak, notes, and settings on this device.
