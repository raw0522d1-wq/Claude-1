## App Store Connect

### Name (30 max)

The Gardener

_If Apple says the name is taken, use: The Gardener: Husband Daily_

### Subtitle (30 max)

Daily prayer for husbands

### Promotional text (170 max)

Start each day praying over your wife and kids by name, then tend three small tasks that build a stronger marriage and home.

### Description (4000 max)

The Gardener is a daily rhythm for husbands who want to love, lead, and pray for their families with intention.

Each morning opens with a verse from Proverbs and a prayer to pray over your wife and children by name. Then you get three simple tasks for the day, drawn from a weekly rhythm of worship, encouragement, service, and pursuit. Every task comes with practical ideas, so you always know what to do next.

EVERY MORNING
• A verse from Proverbs and a written prayer, personalized with your family's names
• 62 prayers that rotate, so none repeats for two months

EVERY DAY
• Three tasks for husbands and fathers, each with four ideas to get it done
• Tasks adjust automatically if you don't have children
• A gentle reminder every two hours until the day's tasks are done, then quiet

YOUR GARDEN
• A streak that grows like a plant, from Sprout to Cedar
• A 12-week view of your consistency
• Milestones that mark real faithfulness

JOURNAL
• Write down answered prayers, conversations, and what you notice
• Tag notes for your wife, kids, prayer, gratitude, or growth

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (public domain).

### Review notes (4000 max)

No sign-in is required and all data stays on the device. The app schedules local reminder notifications every two hours between the user's chosen hours until the day's three tasks are checked off. To see the full flow: finish the three-step setup, tap Amen on the morning prayer, then check off tasks on the Today tab.
