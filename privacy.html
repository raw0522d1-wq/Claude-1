The Gardener

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

In short: The Gardener doesn't collect, send, or sell any of your information. There's no account, no ads, and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The names you enter (yours, your wife's, and your children's), your reminder hours, which tasks you've checked off, your streak, and your journal notes. All of it is saved only in the app's storage on your device. None of it is sent to us or anyone else.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. They don't pass through any server, and you can turn them off in the app's settings or your phone's settings at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, usage data, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
  • We don't share or sell data, because we never receive any.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and choose Erase everything, or uninstall the app. Either one removes all of your information from the device.

Children

The Gardener is made for adults. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
