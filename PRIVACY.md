# FORGE Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-06_

FORGE ("the app") is designed to keep your data on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing is transmitted to us.** The app has no server and no analytics.

Data you create in the app — your profile (name, age, height, weight),
workout logs, check-in/check-out photos, uploaded workout videos, streaks and
milestones — is stored **only on your device**, using your device's local app
storage. It never leaves your phone unless you export or share it yourself.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used solely for workout check-in/check-out photos and for
recording workout example clips, at your request. Photos and videos are saved
to the app's local storage on your device. You can delete them at any time
from the Progress and Clips screens, or remove everything with "Reset
Everything".

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) —
we never see your payment details. If store billing is handled through a
billing provider (e.g. RevenueCat), an anonymous purchase identifier is used
to validate your subscription; no personal profile data is attached to it.

## Data deletion

Delete the app, or use "Reset Everything" in the Progress tab, and all app
data is permanently removed from your device.

## Children

FORGE is not directed at children under 13.
